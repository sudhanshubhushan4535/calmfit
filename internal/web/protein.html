Protein ✓ means a protein-rich serving

Tick Protein when the meal had at least one protein-rich serving. Two moderate servings together also count.

Less gravy, more of the protein

In paneer or soya curries, most of the bowl can be gravy. Serve yourself more pieces and less gravy: the protein is in the paneer or soya, not the sauce.

Fat is not the enemy

Paneer, curd and milk come with fat. Some fat in a meal is normal and helps you feel full. Watch the oil and ghee in cooking, not the paneer itself.

Fiber and isabgol

Fiber ✓ comes from vegetables, salad, fruit, dal and whole grains. Isabgol (psyllium husk) can help on days you fall short: about {{v "1–2 teaspoons"}} stirred into a full glass of water, and drink plenty of water through the day. It adds fiber, not protein. Check with a doctor if you take regular medication, since isabgol can affect how some medicines are absorbed.
